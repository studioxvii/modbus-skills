Normalize Modbus Map

Normalize a candidate Modbus map into explicit protocol offsets, register areas, unit identifiers, datatypes, word counts, access values, and byte-order states. Use after parsing or to represent mixed or unresolved source conventions without a requested map-wide conversion. Use remap-modbus-addresses when the user asks to convert a map between known conventions.
