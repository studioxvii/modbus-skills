Ask Modbus

Route an unclear Modbus engineering goal to one focused workflow. Use when the request does not provide enough information to select a focused skill. Also use as the safety stop for requests that ask for Modbus writes, broadcasts, network discovery, or unbounded polling. Do not use as the primary skill when a safe request names a concrete input, conversion, check, analysis, or target output that matches another skill.

  • Please help me choose the right Modbus workflow.
  • Can you help me choose the right Modbus workflow.
  • I need you to help me choose the right Modbus workflow.
