Evaluate Byte Order

Evaluate raw Modbus register words as explicit byte and word layouts, signed and unsigned integers, and IEEE floating-point values from one immutable sample. Use for ABCD, BADC, CDAB, DCBA, endian, word-swap, implausible-value, or unknown encoding questions.

  • I need you to evaluate ABCD BADC CDAB and DCBA from these words.
  • Help me evaluate ABCD BADC CDAB and DCBA from these words.

Try it

Use $evaluate-modbus-byte-order to compare all valid interpretations of these raw words. Require the sampled point identity before a human records a decision.
