Modbus Skills

Determine byte order from a one-read probe

Collect one raw sample with capture-sample when words are missing, then confirm the layout.

Steps

  1. capture-sample: probe-request/v1 → modbus-tool-pack/v1
  2. external-gate: modbus-tool-pack/v1 → capture/v1
    Run one bounded read and save the returned raw 16-bit words with complete sample identity. Do not scan or write.
  3. workflow: confirm-byte-order: modbus-map/v1, capture/v1 → modbus-map/v1

Stop conditions

  • no probe identity when raw words are missing
  • no raw words after the probe gate
  • ambiguous sample identity
  • no human confirmation
