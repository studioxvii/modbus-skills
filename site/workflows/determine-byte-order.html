Modbus Skills

Determine byte order from supplied raw words

Compare explicit layouts from one immutable raw sample and record human confirmation.

Steps

  1. check-byte-order: capture/v1 → modbus-byte-order-evidence/v1
  2. human-gate: modbus-map/v1, modbus-byte-order-evidence/v1 → modbus-review-decisions/v1
    Present eliminated candidates, the complete remaining shortlist, and distinguishing evidence in one decision. Confirm one explicit layout and keep the raw sample and all candidates.
  3. apply-review: modbus-map/v1, modbus-review-decisions/v1, modbus-byte-order-evidence/v1 → modbus-map/v1

Stop conditions

  • no raw words
  • ambiguous sample identity
  • no human confirmation
